## Text Books & References

### Text Books
1. **Mastering Cloud Computing** — Rajkumar Buyya, Christian Vecchiola, S. Thamarai Selvi, McGraw Hill Education. (Primary reference for Units I, II, III).
2. **Cloud Computing (Fundamentals, Industry Approach and Trends)** — Rishabh Sharma, Wiley. (Reference for Cloud concepts, migration, economics, and service delivery).

### Reference Books
1. **Distributed Systems: Principles and Paradigms** — Andrew S. Tanenbaum & Maarten van Steen, Pearson. (Foundational reference for Unit I distributed systems, architectures, and middleware).
2. **Cloud Computing: Concepts, Technology & Architecture** — Thomas Erl, Pearson. (Comprehensive reference for Units II & III cloud mechanisms, SLA, SOA, and multi-tenancy).
